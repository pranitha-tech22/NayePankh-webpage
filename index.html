Our Work

At NayePankh Foundation, we are dedicated to creating meaningful impact at the grassroots level by supporting education, empowerment, and community development.

📚 Education Support

We support children from low-income communities by providing access to quality education.

🍱 Food Distribution

We conduct regular food drives to serve meals to underprivileged families and individuals, ensuring no one sleeps hungry.

💪 Women Empowerment

We organize awareness campaigns and support systems that encourage women.
